Home Office

Home office make over

I wanted to make my home office more functional. I needed more desk space to work on projects.

I also wanted to create a space that provided more motivation. I wanted to paint the walls and fill the room with items that inspired and created joy and excitement when you looked at it.

The current space was very blah. The white walls didn't provide any inperation. And the bookshelves had become boring.

One day I was sitting in the office and I had the thought, instead of having that wall of bookcases, it would be great to have a workbench along that entire wall.
