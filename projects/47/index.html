This video is the first in a series of videos that I am calling tools for the maker's toolbelt. The idea is to provide techniques or "Tools" that you can use in your projects to give them a new feature or wow factor. These tools are not meant to be stand alone projects, but to be used as part of a larger project. Think of them as widgets that you can build off of. The first tool in my maker's toolbelt is Alexa voice control
