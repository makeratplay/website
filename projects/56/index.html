Take two on the wings

A Second Chance

The Winter Guard decided to use one of the wing props from the Marching Show but needed some modifications. This provided me with an opportunity to improve my design for hanging the winds onto the metal frame. During the marching session, after assembling the props a couple of times, I had thoughts on a better design that would make it much easier to assemble the wings. However, at that point in the marching session, it didn't make sense to make the changes. Now that the Winter Guard wants a set of the wings as props, I can finally apply those new ideas.

The main change that the Winter Guard needed was for the prop frame to be easily moved in and out of the gym. Therefore, the frame needed to be shortened to fit through a door. Additionally, I wanted to modify the way the wings attached to the frame because the previous method was not as stable as I would have liked. It also required Velcro, which was consistently getting pulled off the foam each time the props were dismantled.
