About Me

Hello! I'm Michael Hawkins, a software engineer, maker, and lifelong tinkerer.

How it started

My fascination with programming began around age ten with a Commodore VIC-20. The joy of making something work has stayed with me ever since.

Software and systems

My career has centered on building software, including technology for healthcare organizations. I care about understanding the work people actually do and building technology that helps them.

Making things

Woodworking, metal fabrication, electronics, and programming are different ways to turn ideas into real things. The project gallery is where I share finished work.

Making together

I have spent years volunteering to design and build large marching-band props for The Colony High School Band, combining fabrication, creative problem-solving, and teamwork.

What I am exploring

There is almost always a project on my bench. See the Workbench for current experiments.
